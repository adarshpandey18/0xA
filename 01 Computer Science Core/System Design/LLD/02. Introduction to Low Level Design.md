# Introduction to Low Level Design

## Guiding Principles

Guiding principles are fundamental rules that software engineers follow to write better and maintainable code. While they can be broken in special cases, they are derived from practical experience and should generally be adhered to.

**Examples**: SOLID principles, proper use of inheritance, composition over inheritance, separation of concerns.

---

## Design Patterns

Design patterns are proven solutions to common software design problems. They act as reusable blueprints that guide how to structure and organize code effectively.

They are not code themselves but standardized approaches to recurring design challenges.
